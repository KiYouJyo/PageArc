# Code of Conduct

PageArc is a public project for reading and managing ebooks. We welcome constructive bug reports, accessibility feedback, translations, documentation improvements, and technical contributions.

Participants must be respectful, specific, and mindful that issue reports can contain private book data. Do not post credentials, WebDAV passwords, personal information, copyrighted book contents, or private signing material. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberately malicious reports, and attempts to expose private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, close, or remove contributions that do not follow these rules and may limit participation when necessary to protect the project or its users. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner profile rather than escalating them in a public thread.
